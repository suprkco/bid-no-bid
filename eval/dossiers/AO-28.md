## Contract notice (TED)

- Contracting authority: Swedish Defence Materiel Administration (FMV)
- Title: Hydrophone arrays for coastal surveillance stations
- Procedure: negotiated procedure with prior publication, Directive 2009/81/EC
- Estimated value: SEK 22 000 000 excluding VAT (approx. EUR 1 900 000)
- Duration: 24 months
- Place of performance: supplier premises; installation in the Stockholm archipelago
- Time limit for receipt of requests to participate: 2027-01-11, 12:00 CET
- Languages in which tenders may be submitted: English, Swedish
- Lots: no

## Qualification requirements

### Section 3: Technical capacity

Candidates shall provide at least one reference for the design or delivery of underwater acoustic sensors within the last five years.

### Section 4: Protective security

The contract is subject to a security protection agreement (säkerhetsskyddsavtal) at level 2. All personnel taking part in design, installation or maintenance shall undergo Swedish security vetting and shall be Swedish citizens. Foreign security clearances are not accepted as equivalent for this contract.

### Section 6: Award criteria

1. Quality: 60 %
2. Price: 40 %

## Statement of work (extracts)

### 3. Installation

Arrays shall be installed by the contractor's personnel at three coastal stations, under the supervision of the Swedish Navy.
