## Contract notice (TED)

- Contracting authority: Belgian Defence, Directorate General Material Resources
- Title: Acoustic ranging data processing chain for the naval acoustic test range
- Procedure: negotiated procedure with prior publication, Directive 2009/81/EC
- Estimated value: EUR 600 000 excluding VAT
- Duration: 18 months
- Place of performance: supplier premises; installation at Zeebrugge naval base
- Time limit for receipt of requests to participate: 2026-12-22, 11:00 CET
- Languages in which tenders may be submitted: English, French, Dutch
- Lots: no

## Selection and award

### Section 3: Selection criteria

Candidates shall provide at least one reference in underwater acoustic measurement or sonar signal processing delivered within the last five years.

### Section 4: Security

The contract is unclassified. Access to the naval base requires a standard access badge, issued on presentation of an identity document.

### Section 6: Award criteria

1. Technical value: 70 %
2. Price: 30 %

## Statement of work (extracts)

### 2. Scope

The contractor shall deliver a processing chain that measures the radiated noise signatures of ships crossing the range, generates calibrated reports and archives the measurements.

### 4. Warranty

The contractor shall provide a 24-month warranty with remote support; on-site interventions are scheduled on request.
