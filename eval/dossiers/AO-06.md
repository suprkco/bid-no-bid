## Contract notice (TED)

- Contracting authority: Ministry of Defence of the Netherlands, Defence Materiel Organisation
- Title: Sonobuoy acoustic data processing software for maritime patrol operations
- Procedure: restricted procedure, Directive 2009/81/EC (defence and security)
- Estimated value: EUR 1 100 000 excluding VAT
- Duration: 20 months
- Place of performance: supplier premises; acceptance tests at Den Helder naval base
- Time limit for receipt of tenders: 2027-01-08, 14:00 CET
- Languages in which tenders may be submitted: English, Dutch
- Lots: no

## Instructions to tenderers

### Section 3: Selection criteria

Tenderers shall demonstrate at least one reference in the field of underwater acoustic signal processing or sonobuoy systems delivered within the last five years.

Tenderers shall have an average annual turnover of at least EUR 2 000 000 over the last three financial years.

### Section 4: Security

The contract involves information classified up to the level Stg. GEHEIM (equivalent to SECRET). A Facility Security Clearance issued by the national security authority of an EU Member State with which the Netherlands has a bilateral security agreement is accepted as equivalent to a Dutch clearance. Personnel with access to classified information shall hold an equivalent personnel security clearance of their own Member State.

### Section 6: Award criteria

1. Quality: 65 %
2. Price: 35 %

## Statement of work (extracts)

### 2. Scope

The software shall process passive and active sonobuoy data in real time, provide detection and tracking functions and run on the existing mission computers.

### 5. Language

All documentation shall be delivered in English.

## Special conditions (extracts)

### Article 10: Prices

Prices are fixed for the duration of the contract (20 months).
